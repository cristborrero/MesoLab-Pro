# DESIGN REFERENCE: Stripe Style

Stripe represents the gold standard in clean, developer-focused, yet consumer-friendly product design. Key attributes include pixel-perfect grids, rich gradients, and smooth interactive states.

---

## KEY STYLISTIC ATTRIBUTES

### 1. Typography & Grid
- Pair clean sans-serif typography (e.g., Sohne, Inter) with generous margins.
- Strict 4px or 8px vertical grid. Everything aligns perfectly.

### 2. Colors & Gradients
- Primary brand surfaces are clean white or light cool greys, punctuated by vibrant accents (e.g., deep purples, neon blues, oranges).
- Gradients are complex, smooth, and multi-colored, rather than simple linear fades.

### 3. Shadows & Borders
- Use multi-layered shadows to create realistic depth:
  `shadow-[0_1px_3px_rgba(0,0,0,0.1),0_10px_20px_rgba(0,0,0,0.05)]`
- Borders are very thin and subtle: `border-slate-100` or `border-neutral-200`.

### 4. Interactive Feedback
- Buttons feature a subtle upward lift on hover, micro-scaling, and crisp active states.
- Forms show smooth transitions for border colors and ring glows on focus.
