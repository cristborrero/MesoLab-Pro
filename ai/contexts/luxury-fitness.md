# INDUSTRY CONTEXT: Luxury Fitness & Wellness

This product serves high-end fitness clients, elite athletes, and luxury wellness members.

The interface must communicate:
- premium status and exclusivity
- high performance and discipline
- bodily mastery and wellness science
- aspirational lifestyle
- sophistication

Avoid:
- budget gym aesthetics (neon yellow overload, cluttered schedules, low-quality stock photos)
- aggressive sales tactics (huge red "sale" badges, amateur popups)
- generic layout patterns

The experience should feel closer to:
- premium lifestyle and athletic brands (e.g., Equinox, Alo Yoga, District Vision, WHOOP)
- boutique hotel wellness brands
- high-end luxury watch interfaces

Design guidelines:
1. **High-contrast, moody aesthetics**: Rich dark modes (`bg-[#0a0a0a]`), refined borders (`border-neutral-800`), and gold or deep emerald/bronze accents.
2. **Editorial typography**: Elegant serif headers (e.g., Playfair Display) paired with clean geometric body typography (e.g., Inter, DM Sans).
3. **Immersive visuals**: High-quality lifestyle/athletic photography, large immersive media containers, and smooth view transitions.

Users should feel:
"This is an exclusive, high-performance experience designed for my lifestyle."
