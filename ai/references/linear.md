# DESIGN REFERENCE: Linear Style

Linear is the industry leader in dark-mode performance design, built for keyboard efficiency, absolute speed, and high-density, professional utility.

---

## KEY STYLISTIC ATTRIBUTES

### 1. Palette & Dark Mode
- True dark backgrounds (`#080808` or `#0b0b0b`) paired with slightly lighter surfaces (`#121212` or `#161616`) for cards and modals.
- Accents are minimal, using neon highlights (e.g., violet, electric blue) sparingly.

### 2. Spacing & Borders
- Grid layouts are dense, with tight margins (8px to 16px padding within components).
- Borders are extremely thin and distinct: `border-neutral-800` or `border-[#222]`.
- Corner radiuses are small (e.g., `rounded-md` or `rounded-lg`).

### 3. Keyboard Integration & Focus
- Every action has visible keyboard shortcut hints (e.g., `⌘K`, `Esc`, `Tab`).
- High focus on selection states—active items get subtle glowing borders or left-accent lines.

### 4. Icons & Detail
- Simple, high-quality, 16px monoline icons.
- Micro-interactions (collapsing panels, item reordering) occur instantly without slowing down the user.
