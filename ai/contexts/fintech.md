# INDUSTRY CONTEXT: Fintech & Financial Systems

This product serves business owners, investors, and consumers managing capital.

The interface must communicate:
- absolute security and compliance
- precision and accuracy
- professional transparency
- low risk and reliability
- data clarity

Avoid:
- informal and cute visual styles (childish emojis, playful fonts)
- confusing layouts where financial data is hidden
- micro-copy that is vague or ambiguous

The experience should feel closer to:
- leading fintech products (e.g., Stripe, Wise, Mercury, Wealthfront)
- professional banking platforms
- stock market terminal layouts (restrained version)

Design guidelines:
1. **Perfect alignment and columns**: Strict table grids, right-aligned numbers, monospaced tabular numerals (`font-mono` or `font-feature-settings: 'tnum'`) for amounts.
2. **Clear green/red indicators**: Reserved purely for positive/negative change, profit/loss, and status codes.
3. **Frictionless forms**: Super clean inputs, secure verification fields, transparent fee calculations shown dynamically.

Users should feel:
"My capital is safe, and I have perfect visibility over my finances."
