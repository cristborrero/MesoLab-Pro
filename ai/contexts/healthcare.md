# INDUSTRY CONTEXT: Healthcare & Clinical Aesthetics

This product serves aesthetic professionals and medical professionals.

The interface must communicate:
- clinical trust
- scientific rigor
- product authenticity
- professionalism
- safety
- regulatory confidence

Avoid:
- beauty salon aesthetics (pink pastel overflows, amateur glittery animations, generic spa stock photos)
- cheap ecommerce styling (aggressive discount banners, countdown timers, "buy now" flashing animations)
- aggressive marketing copy ("lose weight fast", miracle cure claims)

The experience should feel closer to:
- premium medical brands (e.g., Allergan, Galderma)
- laboratory suppliers
- healthcare technology companies (e.g., Oscar Health, Forward)

Design guidelines:
1. **Clean sterile layouts**: Lots of negative space, crisp white or cool grey backgrounds, precise borders.
2. **Clinical typography**: Use clear sans-serifs, and monospace labels (e.g., Space Grotesk) for ingredients, volumes, and technical specifications.
3. **Traceability & safety**: Always show certifications, ingredients list, and safety protocols prominently.

Users should feel:
"I can trust these products with my patients."
