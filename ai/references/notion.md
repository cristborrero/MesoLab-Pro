# DESIGN REFERENCE: Notion Style

Notion represents functional simplicity and workspace utility. The design relies on clean structure, monospaced accents, custom icons, and a highly interactive, document-centric layout.

---

## KEY STYLISTIC ATTRIBUTES

### 1. Typography & Accents
- Clean sans-serif primary text paired with monospaced accents for metadata or technical tags.
- Use of icons or emojis to represent items, adding warmth and rapid visual categorization.

### 2. Colors
- Very light gray or clean white backgrounds.
- Color is used sparingly, primarily as text highlights or background blocks (e.g., yellow, blue, green highlights) to mark key quotes or callouts.

### 3. Layout Structure
- Highly flexible, block-based structures.
- Cards have thin, light borders with no shadow to feel lightweight and document-like.

### 4. Usability
- Minimal menus. Interactive controls (like delete, edit, drag handle) only appear on hover, reducing cognitive load when reading.
