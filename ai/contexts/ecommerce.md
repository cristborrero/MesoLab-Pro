# INDUSTRY CONTEXT: Modern E-Commerce & Retail

This product serves consumers and business purchasers looking to buy goods online.

The interface must communicate:
- high product desirability and value
- frictionless purchase path (no roadblocks)
- transparency (shipping, fees, return policies)
- merchant trust and safety
- simplicity

Avoid:
- cluttered retail layouts (too many badges, popups, banner overlays)
- dark patterns (forced subscription checkboxes, hidden fees)
- slow loading times caused by unoptimized media

The experience should feel closer to:
- modern premium storefronts (e.g., Shopify Hydrogen, Gymshark, Apple Store, luxury boutiques)
- minimal checkout flows (e.g., Shop Pay)

Design guidelines:
1. **Product-first layouts**: Large, high-quality media galleries, minimal text clutter next to pricing and CTA.
2. **Clear variation selectors**: Large tap targets for sizes/colors, instant price updates on selection.
3. **Optimized checkout**: Single-page checkout, clear cart summary, transparent tax and shipping fees, visible secure payment badges.

Users should feel:
"I can find what I want, buy it in seconds, and know exactly when it will arrive."
