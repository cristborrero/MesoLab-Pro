# DESIGN REFERENCE: Apple Style

Apple represents premium consumer design, built on absolute minimalism, bold editorial typography, generous whitespace, and rich media assets.

---

## KEY STYLISTIC ATTRIBUTES

### 1. Grid & Whitespace
- Massive margins. Sections have breathing room, letting the product image stand alone.
- Off-center, asymmetric grids that direct focus.

### 2. Typography
- Highly stylized headers using native fonts (e.g., San Francisco, Inter, DM Sans).
- Large, bold headlines paired with small, light-grey body copy.
- Titles carry tight letter-spacing for a modern feel.

### 3. Media & Contrast
- Edge-to-edge photography, high-resolution product closeups, and clean transparent PNGs.
- The interface acts as a quiet frame; the product is the hero.

### 4. Simplicity
- Zero decorative lines. Divisions are made through whitespace and subtle contrast changes (e.g., white cards on light gray backgrounds).
- Navigation is minimal, containing only absolute essentials.
